The cafe

Handed on, not closed down.

Angie Whitworth ran Eastend for seventeen years. When she retired in 2025, Donna Priestley took it on and carried straight on with it.

Donna went from selling savoury pancakes out of a gazebo, to a food van, to owning the place. Her words: "Our focus is on good tasting, fresh food made from scratch when we can."

The building sits at the east end of Marine Parade beside the old Gaiety Theatre, looking over the Wairoa River. Turquoise ceiling, mismatched painted chairs, local photography on the walls with the artist's name and price beside each one.
