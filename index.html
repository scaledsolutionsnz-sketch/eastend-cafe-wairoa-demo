The cafe

Twenty years on Marine Parade, and still going.

Eastend has been part of Wairoa for about two decades. In 2025 Donna Priestley took it on from Angie Whitworth, who had run it for seventeen years, and carried straight on.

Donna went from selling savoury pancakes out of a gazebo, to a food van, to owning the place. As she puts it: “Our focus is on good-tasting, fresh food made from scratch when we can.”

The building sits at the east end of Marine Parade next to the historic Gaiety Theatre, looking out over the Wairoa River. There is a koru laid into the entrance floor in mosaic and old cutlery. You will notice it on the way in.

The cabinet

Havana coffee, and food made here.

Organic Havana beans through the machine all day. The cabinet changes, but there is usually chicken slaw, sausage rolls, eggs bene, a beef burger, and something Eshay has baked that morning.

Breakfast

Eggs bene, homemade granola, corn fritters, pancakes.

Lunch

Chicken slaw, beef burgers, quiche, salads and pies that change through the week.

Gluten free & vegan

Licensed

On the walls

Local work, hung and for sale.

The walls at Eastend double as a small gallery for Hawke's Bay photographers and artists. Lake and coast prints, framed and on canvas, with the artist's name and price beside each one.

Come for the coffee, leave with something for the hallway.
