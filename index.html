The cafe

The one everybody stops at on the way through.

Eastend sits on Marine Parade, right where State Highway 2 runs through Wairoa. Locals treat it as the front room of the town, and anyone driving the Napier–Gisborne road learns quickly that this is the place to pull over.

Big windows, a teal ceiling, timber beams and a counter that fills up from eight in the morning. Come in for a flat white, stay for breakfast.

On the walls

Local work, hung and for sale.

The walls at Eastend double as a small gallery for Hawke's Bay photographers and artists. Lake and coast prints, framed and on canvas, with the artist's name and price beside each one.

Come for the coffee, leave with something for the hallway.
