The cafe

Handed on, not closed down.

Angie Whitworth ran Eastend for seventeen years. When she retired in 2025, Donna Priestley took it on and carried straight on with it.

Donna went from selling savoury pancakes out of a gazebo, to a food van, to owning the place.

"Our focus is on good tasting, fresh food made from scratch when we can." Donna Priestley, owner

The building sits at the east end of Marine Parade beside the old Gaiety Theatre, looking over the Wairoa River. Turquoise ceiling, mismatched painted chairs, and local photography on the walls with the artist's name and price beside each one.

What's on

What is in today.

It changes through the week. Eshay fills the cabinet on a Monday and it is usually empty again by Tuesday.

Mornings

Breakfast

Eggs bene, homemade granola, corn fritters and pancakes, on until the kitchen turns over to lunch.

Middle of the day

Lunch

Chicken slaw, beef burgers, quiche, salads and pies that change through the week.

Havana coffee

From the cabinet

Filled croissants, slices, sausage rolls and cakes. The big sausage rolls go early, so get in.

Dietaries

Gluten free & vegan

Usually something in the cabinet, and if there is not, the kitchen will make you something.

Licensed

Beer & wine

The bar side runs a fire in winter and a slow lunch by the river the rest of the year.

Functions

Catering

Platters and finger food for functions around Wairoa. Give Donna a ring and talk it through.
